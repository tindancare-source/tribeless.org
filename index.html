Who We Are

Advancing Holistic & Inclusive Community Development

Tribeless GH Heritage LBG is a Ghanaian non-profit dedicated to delivering culturally relevant programmes that expand access to quality health services, strengthen education, promote inclusive economic growth, and build lasting social impact.

  • ✓ Maternal & Mental Health — Community-based and digital interventions that reach underserved populations.
  • ✓ Education Access — Culturally grounded curricula and redistribution of learning materials.
  • ✓ Economic Empowerment — Skills development and sustainable livelihood programmes for youth.
  • ✓ Partnerships & Sustainability — Mobilising resources and building local capacity for scale.

What We Do

Our Key Initiatives

Each programme responds to a real challenge witnessed in Ghana's communities — from mental health stigma to broken hospital equipment.

Child Safety

Safe Passage to School Project

Provides life jackets and water safety education to school children who travel by boat in water transport-dependent communities, preventing drowning and improving safe education access.

Learn More
Mental Health

Beyond the Blanket / MindNest

Promotes early intervention, reduces stigma, and improves access to mental health care through digital platforms (MindNest App) for students, pregnant women, trauma survivors, and underserved communities.

Learn More
Healthcare

Hospital Assets Recovery Initiative (HARI)

Works with TVET institutions and artisans to repair non-functional hospital equipment, creating technical training and employment opportunities while improving patient care.

Learn More
Education

Books Reborn

Collects, refurbishes, and redistributes books to underserved schools and communities, supporting libraries and promoting a reading culture across Ghana.

Learn More
Culture & Arts

Live Journey Initiative

Promotes youth engagement through cultural education and the creative arts, supporting cultural facilitators and creating employment in the cultural and creative industries.

Learn More

Why It Matters

Why Tribeless Matters Now

Ghana has made progress — yet many communities still face barriers that limit opportunity and wellbeing. Tribeless responds with practical, community-driven solutions.

🧠

Mental Health Access

Reducing stigma and improving access to mental health support through community-based and digital interventions.

📚

Learning Equality

Ensuring underserved children have access to the educational materials they need to succeed.

🏥

Resilient Health Systems

Restoring broken hospital equipment to improve care for mothers, newborns, and all patients.

💼

Youth Employment

Opening vocational and creative pathways for young people to build dignified, sustainable livelihoods.

🚤

Child Safety

Protecting children in water-dependent communities with life jackets and water safety education.

🤝

Partnership & Scale

Collaborating with government, development partners, and civil society for lasting impact at scale.
